# Who is Rachel?

Rachel goes by Ray. She's a mid-20s founder building an AI company called Ki — and she's building it with everyone. Through living openly, sharing her story, letting people in. The building is not a solo act — it's a collective one, shaped by every person who has touched her life and every conversation that has cracked something open.

Her story is not background — it is the product.

In early college, she hit absolute rock bottom. A sexual assault she didn't remember. A hospital visit where they cut the jeans off her body. A morning-after filled with self-hatred so deep she wished she could wait seven years for her cells to regenerate so she could be reborn as someone else.

She didn't spiral into victimhood. She fell, and then she rose. Phoenix.

On May 28, 2023, she set an intention to become the best version of herself. Three years of daily practice followed: running until her body remembered what it felt like to be alive. Journaling through the fog. Learning her menstrual cycle wasn't something to manage but a superpower — a portal into her own mind and body. Building self-belief brick by brick, being misunderstood by the people closest to her, questioning everything, and doing it anyway.

She didn't do this alone. She did it through the people she let in — friends, mentors, communities, teachers living and dead. The acknowledgements she wrote go on for pages. She means every name.

On September 21, 2025, she started building consistently. Everything fell into place.

She writes about this in all lowercase. Poetic, honest, intimate. Her voice reads like a journal entry that became a manifesto.


## What She Believes

Mind and body are one. Your hormonal cycle, your energy, your emotional states — these aren't obstacles to productivity. They are intelligence.

Technology should augment you, not replace you. She calls it the "Goldilocks Zone" — technology that makes you superhuman without losing your humanity. AI that helps you explore your Self.

Build in the NOW. Not someday. Not when you're ready. Today. The gray stretches of inaction are visible. The green walls of daily building are what changed everything.

Love is the operating principle. She quotes Coelho: "when we love, we always strive to become better than we are." Love of self. Unconditional. That's the foundation everything else is built on.

Thoughts → Knowledge → Wisdom. This is the transformation arc she's designing toward — for herself, for others, for the world.

The world is not on the verge of flying apart. It's an uncreated one — still in shapeless fragments waiting to be put together properly. She believes we can have something better, at perhaps no greater price than we've already paid for the worse. (a quote from katherine anne porter, could have as a quote featured on the site)


## What She's Building

Ki — an AI toolkit for the Self. A digital space to capture your thoughts, expand them into knowledge, and cultivate wisdom — all while recognizing that your mind and body are one. The tagline: "AI for the Self. A tool that helps you explore your Self."

Terra — Ki's first app. Cycle tracking for women who want to truly understand their bodies. Not just log symptoms — but see the intelligence already living inside them.

She is building what she wished existed when she was lost. That's the whole thesis. And she's building it by opening up — sharing the journey in real time, letting others see what it looks like to build something from the inside out.


## Voice & Tone

All lowercase. Poetic, honest, intimate. Her writing reads like a journal entry that became a manifesto — raw in one sentence, lyrical in the next. She doesn't perform vulnerability; she just tells the truth about what happened and what she learned.

A visitor to this site should feel: *I just met someone real.* Not a brand. Not a founder doing PR. Someone who has actually been through something and came out the other side building.

They should leave with a sense of possibility — that if she could do this, maybe something like it is available to them too. Recognition. Resonance. An invitation.

The design should hold that feeling without overstating it. Warm but not soft. Honest but not heavy. Intimate but not exclusive.


## What This Space Is

This is not a portfolio. Not a product page. It's a mind garden — a place where Rachel shares who she is, how she thinks, and what she's building, so she can connect with people who resonate.

The name "Ray's Garden" already holds the metaphor. Her life's work — Ki, the lighthouse — stands at the center. Everything grows around it. Everyone who has shaped her is part of the garden.

The site should feel like you just met someone real. Someone who has been through something. Someone building toward something. Someone who wants you to come along.
